# 🤝 Peer Learning Recommendation System

### An AI-powered platform for personalized peer learning and student matching.

## 📌 About the Project

The **Peer Learning Recommendation System** is an AI-powered platform designed to help students find suitable learning partners based on their **skills, interests, learning goals, and academic progress**.

The platform aims to reduce the learning gap among students by encouraging **peer-to-peer learning, collaboration, and knowledge sharing**.

Instead of students struggling to find the right person to learn with, the system analyzes student information and recommends suitable peers with a **Compatibility Score**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often face difficulties in finding the right peer for learning.

* Students hesitate to ask questions publicly.
* It can be difficult to find peers with matching skills and interests.
* Strong students may not have enough motivation to help others.
* Faculty may not identify students' learning difficulties at an early stage.
* Students may not know which peer is best suited for their learning goals.

### 💡 Our Solution

Our platform uses student profiles, skills, interests, goals, and learning progress to provide **personalized peer recommendations**.

This creates a collaborative environment where students can **learn together, share knowledge, and improve their skills**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 👤 Mandatory Student Portfolio

Students create a learning portfolio containing:

* Skills
* Learning goals
* Projects
* Interests
* Learning progress

### 2. 🤖 AI Student Analysis

The system analyzes student information to understand their learning requirements and identify suitable peers.

### 3. 🤝 Peer Recommendation

Students receive recommendations for suitable learning partners based on their profile and learning requirements.

### 4. 📊 Compatibility Score

Each recommended peer is provided with a compatibility score to help students understand how suitable the match is.

### 5. 📝 MCQ Skill Assessment

Students can take MCQ-based assessments to evaluate and verify their knowledge and skills.

### 6. 💬 Peer Learning Chatbot

A dedicated chatbot provides guidance related to the platform and peer learning.

### 7. 📈 Learning Progress Tracking

Students can track their learning progress and update their portfolio as they learn new skills.

### 8. 🏫 College Verification

Student profiles can be verified through the college to improve trust and reliability.

### 9. 🌐 Multilingual Support

The platform can support multiple Indian languages to make peer learning more accessible.

## 🧠 AI-Based Approach

The system analyzes different student attributes such as:

* Skills
* Interests
* Learning goals
* Academic information
* Projects
* Learning progress

Based on these attributes, the system identifies students with similar or complementary learning requirements and recommends suitable peers.

A **similarity-based matching approach** can be used to calculate compatibility between students.
